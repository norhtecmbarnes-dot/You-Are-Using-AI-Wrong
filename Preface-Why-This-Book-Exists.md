# Preface — Why This Book Exists

**STATUS: draft for the author to react to.** The one thing here that is not mine to write is the detail of
your own experience — so the story is told in your words and everything I could not know is flagged, not
invented. Look for `⚠️ TO CONFIRM` and fill or cut.

---

I went to India for work. I had time on my hands, so I offered to give a short class on using AI.

I have spent over thirty years in the computer industry. For the last few, I have been building an AI
system of my own — private, running on my own machine, reading my own documents, doing work I actually get
paid for. That is what I showed them. Not a list of prompts. How the thing behaves, and how to work with it
instead of against it.

*⚠️ TO CONFIRM — what you actually covered in the class, who was in the room, and where: a paragraph of
concrete detail here would do more for this preface than anything I can invent. What did you demonstrate?
What surprised them first?*

Afterwards, a student came up and said that **I used AI in a way they had never seen before.**

I have thought about that sentence more than anything else anyone has said about my work, because it was
not really about me. Everyone in that room had access to the same models I do. I am not smarter than they
are and I do not have better tools. What they had not seen was a different *way of holding* the tool.

That is the gap this book is written into — not a shortage of intelligence, or effort, or curiosity. **A
shortage of anyone telling people how these systems actually work.** And the machine itself has every
incentive to keep that quiet, because a tool that says *ask me anything* is easier to sell than one that
says *you bring the knowledge.*

So this is what I want to hand you. Not tactics that will be stale by the time you read them, but the shape
of the thing: what it is, what it cannot do, and how to build with it anyway.

And one thing before we start, because I mean it.

**If you have been using AI wrong, it is not your fault.** Nobody handed you a manual. You were handed a
blinking cursor that promised to answer everything, and you did what any reasonable person does with a
promise. The rest of this book explains why that cursor could never keep its word — and what to build
instead.

## Notes for the author (not part of the preface)

**Why this is a preface and not a chapter.** It has one job: to establish that you are writing from
having *done* the work, and to state the book's stance — *it isn't your fault* — before the reader has any
reason to feel accused. Everything else the book does can be argued. This part has to be *earned*, and the
India story earns it in three sentences.

**The structural move it enables.** The preface now sets up a frame:

> **Preface:** a student says *I've never seen AI used that way.*
> **Final chapter (Ch. 27, "Start here"):** the reader is now the student.

That is the whole book in two bookends, and it costs nothing. The final chapter should close by handing the
reader the same thing you handed that class — a first move they can make the same afternoon.

**What must not happen to it.** Do not let this become a testimonial page or a résumé. The thirty years and
the dashboard are *credentials*, and they go by quickly; the preface is about the student's sentence and
what it revealed. If a sentence here is about how impressive the system is, cut it.

**Tone check.** Warm, plain, first person. No *"little did I know."* No *"and that changed everything."*
The strongest line in the draft is the shortest one — *I used AI in a way they had never seen before* —
and it works precisely because you did not editorialise it.
