# Part 3 — What Right Looks Like

The mechanics are established. The error is named.

This part turns them into practice: how to organize agents, prompts, expertise, and inherited model behavior into systems that produce useful work.

Each chapter here carries a rule from the rules page, and each rule is demonstrated — by code, by a benchmark, or by a worked example the reader can run. The aim is not to make AI look smaller than it is.

The aim is to build a system an organization can rely on.
