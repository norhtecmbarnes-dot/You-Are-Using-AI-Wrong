# Part V — The Promise and the Principle

*(Working title — the author owns the part's name.)*

Two rules bring the book to a close.

The first clears the ground one last time. The promise economy — the books, the TikToks, the YouTube
channels — sells outcomes: make a million dollars with AI, go viral, replace the workforce. A wish
has no ingredients. Work does.

The second lands the argument. Principle and value — not just profit or results. And the way his own
working life used to set them: employee input; management review and decision; a published page in
front of every employee, reminding each one that every action must comply.

The genie is not owed a wish. The work is — and the principle decides what the work is worth.
