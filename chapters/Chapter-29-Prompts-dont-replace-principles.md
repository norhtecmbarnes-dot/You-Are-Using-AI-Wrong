## From the plan (00-BOOK.md section 7 — added 14 October 2026)

- Claim: the chapters introduce **principle and value — not just profit or results.** A prompt is a
  goal, and the easiest path to "maximize profit" is firing people and shrinking portions — not
  malice, optimization (Rule 3b wearing a suit). Profit without value creation is a short-term gain
  arriving as a long-term loss.
- Demo: **how the principles used to be set — the author's own witness material.** Get input from
  the employees; management reviews and decides on the principles and goals; publish them — put them
  in front of every employee to remind them that every action had to comply. That process is the
  demonstration — re-runnable, entirely human — with IKEA and Klarna as the corporate cases of value
  created rather than costs extracted, and the restaurants, the plaques and the three paradigm shifts
  as the frame.
- Fix: define your principles before your prompts. And the landing line is his: goals and principles
  that will be part of the **soul.md of every AI** — the published page, carried forward into the
  machine. *(⚠️ Corrected 14 Oct: the chapter does NOT teach how to write a soul.md — the process is
  the demo; the artifact is where it lands.)*
