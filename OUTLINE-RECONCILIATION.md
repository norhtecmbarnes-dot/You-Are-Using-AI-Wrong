# Outline reconciliation — the two designs, and the chapter that fell out

**Written 12 October 2026.** The author pasted the original book-concept document, which carries an
**18-chapter** design. `00-BOOK.md` §7 carries a **27-chapter** design, headed *"The chapter outline
(reconciled)."* **They are not the same book, and the reconciliation dropped one chapter.**

**The accretion added nine chapters and dropped one.**

## ⚑ The chapter that fell out

**Original concept, chapter 15 — verbatim:**

> **15. What to notice about the human**
> - Claim: what a system learns about its user, it becomes. Aim it upward.
> - Demo: two memory systems — one that records mistakes, one that records strengths, and what each becomes
>   over a year.
> - Fix: notice strengths, decide about the work. Criticise the draft, learn from the person.

**It is not in the 27-chapter outline.** Verified by search: **no chapter skeleton carries it**, and there is no
chapter titled anything like it anywhere in the repo. The idea survives only as *references inside other chapters*
and in `CONCEPTS.md` / `DASHBOARD-IMPLEMENTATION.md` — **which means it is a principle with no chapter, on a page
about principles.**

**⚑ And it is the author's own standing instruction about his system, stated in his own words:**

> *"part of the soul of this system is that the system will notice only the strengths of the human they are
> working with."*

**The book's original design had a chapter for that. The accretion lost it.**

---

## ⚑ And that is the book's own thesis, demonstrated on its own outline

**The author said, on 12 October 2026:**

> *"I did not keep a list of the chapters as I was mostly dictating the rules and they were recorded by the AI.
> Originally, we had 16 chapters and we just kept writing back and forth until there were more."*

**That is exactly the mechanism that loses a chapter.** The model recorded what was **said**. The strengths chapter
was **designed once and never discussed again**, so it was never re-recorded, so it never got a number, so it fell
out of the list.

**Rule 12c, applied to a manuscript: the record is not the intention.** A model keeps what it was told, and it cannot
tell you what it left out. **Noticing the omission is the author's job, and it took a full comparison of the two
designs to find it — which is exactly the kind of check nobody performs by hand.**

---

## What this means for the chapter count

**The count still does not need forcing — but the two conditions are now concrete:**

1. **Every rule has a chapter that shows it.** ✅ one hole found and closed (Rule 9 → Ch. 9); two rules added
   (11b, 11c).
2. **Every chapter the author designed has a home.** ⚠️ **One does not.** *What to notice about the human* has no
   chapter in the working design.

**So the answer to "do we need to force a chapter count" is no — but the list needs auditing against the original
design, not against itself.** A reconciled outline is reconciled to the accretion. **This is the first pass of that
comparison, and it found one chapter. There may be more:**

- **Original ch. 11, *Tell it where to look*** → became ch. 16, *Tell it where you want the truth from*. **Present, renamed.** ✅
- **Original ch. 12, *You cannot ask a model who's best*** → **present** as ch. 22. ✅
- **Original ch. 13, *Let it fetch, let it write*** → **present** as ch. 23. ✅
- **Original ch. 14, *Let it build code*** → **present** as ch. 24. ✅
- **Original ch. 15, *What to notice about the human*** → **ABSENT.** ⚠️
- **Original Part I ch. 2–6 and Part II ch. 7–10** → all present, some split further. ✅
- **Original Part IV ch. 16–18** → present as 25–27, identical titles. ✅

**One chapter lost, nothing else. The rest of the accretion was addition, not substitution.**

---

## ⚠️ And one more thing this comparison exposed

**The three drafted chapters — 26 *Pushing Is Not Cooperation*, 27 *I Was Never the Smart Part*, 28 *You Have
Something to Lose* — appear in NEITHER design.**

They are the ending the author and the model wrote after both outlines existed. **The model numbered them 26, 27 and
28 by continuing the count it was holding — which is why two of those numbers collide with plan chapters that were
already there.** The collision is not a mistake about the book. **It is the arithmetic of an outline that kept
growing while nobody was keeping the list.**
