## 0. ⚠️ TURN WEB RESEARCH OFF (do this first)

Youbooks' generator has a toggle called **"One Click, One Book"**, and its own guidance is *"you should
normally leave this ON."*

**For this book, turn it OFF.**

Web research puts the model's own knowledge and the open web back into the loop — which is exactly what
Rule 1 of your book forbids. **With it off, the only information it has is what you upload.** That is the
whole method, and it is the difference between this tool working and this tool being the thing your book
warns about.
